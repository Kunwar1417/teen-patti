Teen Patti Hisaab

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026 · Published by Never Settle Lab · Applies to the Android app, the iOS app and the web version.

In short: the app collects no personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app is

Teen Patti Hisaab is a scorekeeper for Teen Patti card nights with friends. It keeps track of the boot, bets, the pot, round winners and who owes whom at the end. It does not run any game of chance, it does not handle money, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

Information stored on your device

To remember your game between sessions, the app saves the following only in your device's local storage:

  • Player names and colors you type in
  • Bets, rounds, winners and settlement ticks for the current game
  • Your table settings (boot and pot limit) and whether sound is on

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or tapping "Reset Entire Game & History", deletes it.

Information we collect

None. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The Android and iOS apps include their fonts, so they make no network requests while you use them.

Sharing a settlement

If you tap "WhatsApp" or "Copy" on the settlement screen, the app prepares a text summary (player names and amounts) and hands it to WhatsApp or your clipboard. You choose whether and where to send it. That sharing is handled by WhatsApp or your device, under their own privacy policies.

Web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may log standard technical details such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy. The app itself sends them nothing about your games.

Children

The app is meant for adults tracking a social card game. It is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
